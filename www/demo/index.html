FIRST VISIT

How a first visit
usually works.

You don’t need to know the difference between the groups, or anything about rationality, to turn up at one of their events.

Ask about your first visit
  1. 01

    Pick an event

    A dinner, a discussion or a game. The calendar lists them all.

  2. 02

    Get the practical details

    Check the original announcement for language, preparation, price, access, and registration.

  3. 03

    Say hello to the host

    A short message beforehand makes it easier to find the group when you arrive.

THE GROUPS

Five groups in Munich

Each has its own organisers,
events and way of doing things.

01↗

RATIONALITY

LessWrong / ACX Munich

Papers, essays and blog posts, discussed over dinner twice a month. The LessWrong and Astral Codex Ten community also runs a monthly estimation game with EA Munich.

Dinner discussions · monthly thinking games
LessWrong group page

02↗

EFFECTIVE ALTRUISM

Effective Altruism Munich

Socials, introductory programmes, workshops and talks on using evidence and careful reasoning to help others. Newcomers are welcome, including people coming on their own.

Students & professionals · no prior knowledge needed
eamuenchen.de

AI ADVOCACY

PauseAI Munich

The Munich chapter of PauseAI, which campaigns on the risks of advanced AI. The chapter page describes the group’s approach and how to get involved.

Local chapter · advocacy
pause-ai.de

AI SAFETY

AI Safety Munich Student Club

Reading groups, research and workshops on compute verification and AI governance. The club’s introduction lists its activities, branch leads and membership details.

Student community · research · workshops
The club’s info doc

05↗

PHILOSOPHY

Philosophia Munich

A student-run philosophy society open to everyone, including non-students. Discussions are in English, usually after reading a paper. No academic philosophy background is required.

English · weekly during semester · pre-reading
philosophiamunich.org

The groups announce their own events; this page only collects them.
