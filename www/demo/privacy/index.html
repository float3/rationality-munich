PRIVACY

What this demo
does with data.

Short answer: nothing leaves your browser.

The short version

The signup demo does not send emails, create subscriptions, or submit your entries to a server. Entries stay in the current page’s memory and disappear when you reload or leave the page. The demo stores no preferences in cookies or browser storage.

External links

Organizer announcements, the live mailing list, calendar subscription, and contact links open external services or your own email/calendar app. Their own policies apply. Clicking a contact link prepares an email in your app; this demo does not send it.

Images and fonts

The Munich image is served locally with attribution. The site uses fonts available on your device, without downloading fonts from third-party services.

The live website

The original website has its own policy, including details about its host, server logs, mailing-list processor, and unsubscribe tools. This demo does not replace or verify that policy.

Read the live site’s privacy policy ↗

Before a public launch

The owner should review the privacy information against the actual hosting, mailing-list integration, retention policy, and email provider. This demo deliberately does not claim that a live service is connected.
